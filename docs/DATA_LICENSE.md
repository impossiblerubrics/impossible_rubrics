# Data, results and third-party evidence licensing

## Project-owned material: Apache-2.0

The maintainers license the original benchmark questions, oracle certificates,
fixed answers, annotations, synthetic material and experimental result records
that they own or are authorized to license under the
[Apache License, Version 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
This includes the project-owned selection and scoring records in the optional
reproduction pack. Source code and original project documentation use the same
license. The full license text is included in `LICENSE` at the package root.

This grant applies only to rights held by the maintainers. It does not grant
rights over quoted or incorporated third-party material, including third-party
text embedded in a question, answer, annotation or synthetic example. It does
not claim copyright over facts or other material outside copyright protection.

## Third-party evidence: original rights and terms

Third-party evidence is excluded from the project's Apache-2.0 grant and retains
its original rights and applicable terms. Including text in this dataset does
not relicense that text. Where redistribution is permitted, retain the required
source attribution, license and notices; the project license does not replace
them. Evidence without a verified source license or other redistribution basis
remains marked for review.

The frozen dataset contains 937 evidence passages, including 12 marked synthetic
by the provenance rules. URLs, citations, fetch-verification status, word counts
and text hashes are listed in the
[source inventory](https://github.com/impossiblerubrics/impossible_rubrics/blob/main/docs/source_manifest.json).
`source_license` records upstream licensing information and remains unknown until
verified; it is separate from the Apache-2.0 grant on project-owned material.
A synthetic provenance label does not establish rights in referenced external
content. Public availability, a source URL or successful fetching alone does not
establish a redistribution basis.

The source-rights review is still pending for the 925 passages not marked
synthetic. This status describes an unfinished review, not a determination that
every passage requires separate permission or that none can be redistributed.

## Corrections and preservation

Keep the frozen evidence text and scientific annotations intact when reviewing
rights. A citation-only or rights-resolved subset must be versioned and labeled
as such, rather than described as the complete original benchmark.

For corrections, identify the task ID, document ID and source URL when contacting
the maintainers through the project page. Do not post private personal details
or API credentials in an issue.
